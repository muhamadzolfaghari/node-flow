How to use it

  1. Open the template in the studio.
  2. Change the source or select shapes on the canvas to rename, restyle, and connect them.
  3. Run the autonomous AI Architecture Agent to verify coupling and single points of failure.
  4. Export as SVG, 4K PNG, PDF, PPTX, or standalone HTML, or keep it as a project in your browser.
